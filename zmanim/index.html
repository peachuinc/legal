Privacy Policy

Zmanim - Jewish Prayer Times

Last updated: April 2026

Summary

Zmanim respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All calculations happen on your device.

Location Data

Zmanim requests access to your device location solely to calculate accurate prayer times for your area. Your location data:

  • Is processed entirely on your device
  • Is never sent to any server
  • Is never shared with third parties
  • Is only used when you grant permission

Data Storage

All app data (saved locations, preferences, Daf Yomi progress) is stored locally on your device using Apple's built-in data frameworks. Nothing is transmitted externally.

Analytics & Tracking

Zmanim does not use any analytics services, tracking pixels, or advertising frameworks. We have no way to identify you or monitor your usage.

Subscriptions

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

Zmanim does not integrate with any third-party services. No data leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

Zmanim does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
