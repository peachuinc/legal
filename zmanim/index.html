Privacy Policy

Zmanim - Jewish Prayer Times

Last updated: July 2026

Summary

Zmanim respects your privacy. We do not create user accounts, sell data, show ads, or use tracking. Core zmanim calculations happen on your device. We use limited Firebase Analytics only to understand basic app usage and improve the app.

Location Data

Zmanim requests access to your device location solely to calculate accurate prayer times for your area. Your location data:

  • Is used for on-device zmanim calculations
  • May be sent to Apple's geocoding service to show a city name
  • Is not sent to us
  • Is not shared with advertising services
  • Is not sent to Firebase Analytics as exact coordinates
  • May be reflected in analytics only as coarse region information
  • Is only used when you grant permission

Data Storage

All app data (saved locations, preferences, Daf Yomi progress) is stored locally on your device using Apple's built-in data frameworks. We do not operate an app server for this data.

Analytics & Tracking

Zmanim uses Firebase Analytics to understand basic app usage, such as app opens, paywall views, purchases, reminders, and whether zmanim screens are viewed. We do not use analytics for tracking or advertising, and we do not send names, emails, exact location, searched places, prayer text, or Siddur text.

Analytics data may include product interaction events, a Firebase app/device identifier used for analytics, and coarse location such as region-level information. This analytics data is not linked by us to your identity and is not used for third-party advertising.

You can turn anonymous usage analytics off at any time in Zmanim Settings under Privacy. When analytics are off, Zmanim stops sending Firebase Analytics events.

Subscriptions

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

City search and reverse geocoding are handled by Apple's geocoding service. In-app purchases are handled by Apple. Basic app usage analytics are handled by Firebase Analytics. Zmanim does not include advertising SDKs or developer-operated backend services.

Children's Privacy

Zmanim does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
