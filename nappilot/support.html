FAQ

How do I add a new baby?

Tap the + button on the Today tab. Enter your baby's name and birth date, then tap Add Baby.

How do I log naps or night sleep?

Open your baby's plan and tap Log Sleep. Choose nap or night sleep, set the start and end times, add an optional note, and save the log.

How does NapPilot estimate nap windows?

NapPilot starts with age-aware wake-window ranges, then anchors today's next window from the latest wake-up or usual wake time. Sleep logs help the plan reflect the current day. Sleepy cues and safe sleep guidance should always come first.

Why can I only see 7 days of history?

The free tier focuses on recent sleep history. Upgrade to NapPilot Pro to unlock full history and export tools.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings > your Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device. You can manage or cancel your NapPilot Pro subscription there.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored locally on your device. It is included in your iCloud device backups if you have them enabled. NapPilot does not require an account or cloud sync.
