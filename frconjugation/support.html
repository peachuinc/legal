Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The verb reference is bundled with the app and core lookup works without an account or developer-operated backend.

How do I find a verb?

Open the Verb List tab and search by infinitive. Tap a verb to view its conjugation table and related details.

How do favourites and recent verbs work?

Favourites and recent lookups are stored locally on your device so you can return to useful verbs quickly.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the paywall or Settings screen and use Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original App Store purchase.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings in the app and choose Manage Subscription, or manage subscriptions from your Apple ID account settings.

Can I turn off analytics?

Yes. Open Settings, go to Privacy, and turn off anonymous usage analytics.

Why is a verb or tense missing?

The app's verb database and practice tools are being expanded over time. If you notice missing or incorrect data, email the verb and expected form so it can be reviewed.
