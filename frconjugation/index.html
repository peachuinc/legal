Privacy Policy

French Verb Conjugation

Last updated: July 2026

Summary

French Verb Conjugation is a fast offline French verb reference. We do not create user accounts, sell data, show ads, or use tracking. Verb lookup, favourites, recent verbs, practice progress, and app preferences are stored on your device. When iCloud Sync is active, the learning data described below is also stored privately in your iCloud account so it can be restored after reinstalling the app.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app may store your favourites, recent lookups, practice state, language and appearance preferences, purchase entitlement state, and anonymous analytics preference on your device. This information is used to make the app work and is not sent to a developer-operated backend.

iCloud Sync

French Verb Conjugation uses Apple's CloudKit service and your private iCloud database to sync learning data for app functionality and reinstall recovery. This may include practice progress and history, favourites, recent verbs, and remembered practice setups.

iCloud Sync is enabled by default when iCloud is available. You can turn it off in Settings; the change takes effect after reopening the app. The app remains usable with local storage when iCloud is unavailable or disabled, but iCloud restore is not available while sync is off.

Analytics & Tracking

French Verb Conjugation uses Firebase Analytics to understand basic app usage and improve the app. Analytics may include events such as app opens, tab views, searches by query length, paywall views, purchase flow outcomes, restore outcomes, and whether a feature was used.

We do not use analytics for tracking or advertising. Analytics data is not linked by us to your identity and is not used for third-party advertising. The app does not send names, email addresses, free-form search text, practice answers, mistakes, or custom verb content in analytics events.

Analytics data may include product interaction events, purchase history related to the app, and a Firebase app/device identifier used for analytics. You can turn anonymous usage analytics off at any time in Settings under Privacy.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card information. Apple may provide the app with purchase entitlement information so Pro features can be unlocked or restored.

Third-Party Services

French Verb Conjugation uses Apple's App Store and StoreKit for purchases, iCloud and CloudKit for private learning-data sync, and Firebase Analytics for optional anonymous usage analytics. The app does not include advertising SDKs and does not operate a custom app server.

Data Deletion

You can remove local app data by deleting the app from your device. When iCloud Sync is active, use Delete iCloud Data in Settings to permanently delete synced practice progress and history, favourites, recent verbs, and saved practice setups from both the device and iCloud. You can also turn off iCloud Sync or anonymous usage analytics in Settings.

Children's Privacy

French Verb Conjugation does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
